Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in TechFest?

Students from colleges and universities can participate in the event.

Is registration compulsory?

Yes. Participants must complete the registration form before the event.

How many events can I participate in?

A student can participate in up to two events.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Registration for the main events is free.

Will participants receive certificates?

Yes. All registered participants will receive participation certificates.
