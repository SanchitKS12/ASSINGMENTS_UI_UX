Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in TechFest?

Students from colleges and universities can participate in the event.

Is registration compulsory?

Yes. Participants must complete the online registration form before the event.

How many events can I participate in?

A student can participate in up to two events, depending on the event schedule.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Registration for the main college events is free.

Will participants receive certificates?

Yes. All registered participants will receive participation certificates.

Where will the events take place?

Different competitions will be conducted in the computer labs, seminar hall, auditorium and open stage.
